SYMBOL / CONSTRUCTION STUDY
01 THE UPPER CURRENT 02 THE HEX CORE 03 THE RETURN
  1. 01

    Matching currents, 180° apart.

    The upper and lower currents share the same geometry, related by a half-turn. Their opposing colors give the exchange its direction.

  2. 02

    A stable, six-sided center.

    A rounded flat-top hexagon anchors the currents. Centered within the mark, it gives the music and the performer a firm point of focus.

  3. 03

    Two parts in dialogue.

    The matching return balances the forward current around the hex core. The mark stays compact, clear and recognizable on a desktop.

01 / ROOM TO BREATHE

Clear space = ½x.

Let x equal the hex core’s flat-to-flat height. Keep at least ½x clear around the mark or lockup. On the 512 × 500 master, x ≈ 174.94 and ½x ≈ 87.47 units.

02 / SIZE WITH INTENT

Small mark. Strong signal.

Use the full symbol at 32 px and above. For 16–24 px interfaces, use the simplified symbolic artwork.

03 / KEEP IT TRUE

No stretch. No extras.

Keep the original proportions, current colors and core. Avoid outlines, gradients, shadows or animated logo rotation.

02 / COLOR

Quiet foundation.
Unmistakable signal.

Charcoal and ivory give the music room. A direct, vivid red marks the moments that matter.

07 / RHYTHM & BEHAVIOR

Give every element
a reason to move.

A confident identity can be quiet. Use structure and motion to orient people, then let the content lead.

LAYOUT

A clear 8 px rhythm.

Use a 4 px base unit and 8, 16, 24, 32 and 48 px layout spacing. Give the mark generous editorial space; tighten the rhythm around performance controls.

Aa128.00

HIERARCHY

One clear focal point.

Let display type lead a page. Inside the application, let the music data lead. Keep supporting text comfortably readable.

MOTION

160 ms / 240 ms.

Use 160 ms for feedback, 240 ms for a panel. Respect reduced motion. No perpetual spinning, pulsing or decorative animation.
