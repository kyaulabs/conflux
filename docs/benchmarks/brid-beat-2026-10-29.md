Both hosts started together. Apollo reached the unchanged 1,170-second native corpus limit: 55 analyses completed, the next source preparation was cancelled, and 37 sources remained unattempted. Its alternating labels 1/2 are retained; position 1 supplies the downbeat reference. They supply neither four-beat bars nor phrase truth. Publisher HTTPS ranges admitted 469,990,540 bytes of complete WAVs after exact archive-directory, member-extent, CRC, source-geometry and reference-extent checks. New full-file SHA-256 identities were recorded and rechecked. The full audio archive's MD5 was not computed. The small annotation archive's published checksum was verified before selection. Ten range/admission checks passed locally and on Apollo. Every selected source remained in the denominator; no output-based exclusion or phase/octave fitting was allowed.

The private adapter calls the existing native AudioFile preparer and BpmAnalyzer against the frozen O2 libraries. It retains the actual stereo 48 kHz float32 extent and hash, then invokes the unchanged CPU float32 Final0 worker, source closure and fifteen dependency versions. No analysis-cache hit is allowed. Beat scoring reuses the retained ordered maximum-cardinality/minimum-error matcher: F1 ≥0.90 at 70 ms, matched median ≤35 ms, nearest-rank p95 ≤70 ms, and median known adjacent-tempo relative error ≤3%. Downbeat F1 ≥0.90 is checked separately at 70 ms. Seven scorer checks cover malformed output, wrong bar phase, missing cases, abstention and prohibited shift fitting. Confidence remains Unknown.

All 93 laptop cases met the matched-event median and p95 bounds. Eleven failed beat F1 and eight failed adjacent-tempo accuracy, with overlap. That does not establish the timing of unmatched beats. A separate post-output audit counted 649 four-event and 440 two-event gaps between estimated downbeats, against references with alternating two-position labels. This describes the failure; it does not justify repairing labels, imposing a meter or promoting automatic downbeat/phrase alignment.
